How resale works here

Three steps, all inside the campus network.

1. List it

Add a title, price and condition. Smart Suggestions fills in the category and keywords for you.

2. Someone finds it

Every listing is searchable and filterable. Guests can browse and build a cart before signing up.

3. Wallet settles it

Payment moves from the buyer's campus wallet to the seller's, and both see it in their transactions.
